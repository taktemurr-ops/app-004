# Sleep Universe — English store and privacy draft

**Short description:** Private offline sleep duration journal.

**Description:** Record sleep duration, browse your history and remove entries. English, Turkish and Arabic interface resources are included.

## Data handling — current MVP
- Sleep duration and timestamps are stored on-device with shared_preferences, without encryption.
- No account, analytics, advertising, cloud sync or remote transmission is implemented.
- Individual record deletion exists. Complete data export and delete-all are not yet implemented.
- This is a sleep journal, not a diagnostic tool or medical advice.

## Release blockers
Implement deletion/export and assess secure storage; verify platform backup settings, accessibility, RTL, locale-aware input, regional disclosures and store privacy labels before launch. This draft is **not** a final legal privacy policy.
